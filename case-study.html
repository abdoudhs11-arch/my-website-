01 / BRANDING + SIGNAGE

NO
FILTER.

A bold identity for a place that refuses to blend in.

CLIENTNo Filter
SECTORHospitality
YEAR2026
LOCATIONAlgiers
SERVICESIdentity / Signage
DURATION08 weeks
01 / THE BRIEF

Make the entrance
impossible to miss.

No Filter needed a visual identity with the same energy as its name — direct, playful and impossible to mistake for another place. The challenge was to make a compact footprint feel like a destination.

02 / OUR RESPONSE

A system built
for the street.

We created a high-contrast identity and carried it from the first exterior sign into every physical detail. Oversized type, electric blue, custom fabrication and a tight set of graphic rules made the brand feel coherent at every distance.

03 / THE OUTCOME

A signature
you can see coming.

The finished space turns recognition into instinct. It reads clearly from across the street, photographs naturally and gives the team a flexible system to keep building on.
